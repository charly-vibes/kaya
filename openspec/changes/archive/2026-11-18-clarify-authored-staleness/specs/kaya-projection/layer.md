# kaya.projection — dual-format layer (sidecar)

## Purpose

Documentation as a compilation: deterministic, incremental, with source
maps back to evidence. The managed-block grammar is the contract that lets
documents stay live — generated sections track the ledger while authored
interpretation survives untouched — and stale flagging is what replaces the
silent drift that motivated kaya: the human finds out their interpretation
rests on changed evidence, instead of never finding out. This change adds the
missing half of that contract: what counts as changed, and which human act
clears the flag.
